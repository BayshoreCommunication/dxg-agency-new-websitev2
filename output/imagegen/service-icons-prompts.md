## event-production-management

### Design prompt

Create ONE clean minimalist white outline pictogram for Event Production Management on a genuinely transparent background. This is a UI icon, NOT an illustration: use only a plain rectangular presentation screen on two short supports, an overhead horizontal bar on two slim posts with three tiny circular lights, and three simple audience head-and-shoulder outlines. Do not use lattice trusses, speakers, spotlit rays, stars, tools or complex scenery. Crisp mathematically smooth geometric lines, exactly uniform 20px stroke on a 1024px square, round caps and joins. Pure solid white #FFFFFF strokes, fully transparent interior negative space and fully transparent background. Every white stroke completely solid, continuous and clean with NO distress, speckles, holes, texture, grain, hatching, shadow, glow or gradients. Center overall mark within a 720x720 box, equal 152px margins. Front elevation, symmetrical, generous negative space, legible at 64px. No letters or captions. Premium consistent monoline icon language like a polished UI symbol family. Render just this ONE icon.
