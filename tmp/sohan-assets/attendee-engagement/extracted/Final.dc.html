FAQ

Questions planners ask us.

Is this gamification?

Sometimes a game is the right tool, but it's one option among many. We choose the format after we know what should change and why people will want to take part.

Does every session need to be interactive?

No. Listening and reflecting are often the right choice. We design interaction where it serves the outcome: one session, one stage, or the whole journey.

Do we have to use a specific platform, or an app at all?

No. We work with the tools you already license or recommend options that fit. When an analog approach does the job better, we'll tell you.

Can we customize the tools to match our event brand?

Yes. Templates, screens, apps, and signage carry your colors, logos, and sponsor placements.

How do you make engagement accessible?

Accessibility is part of the design from the start. We plan more than one way to understand, take part, and progress, so people with different abilities, devices, languages, and comfort levels reach the same outcome.

Can you guarantee results?

We guarantee a clear definition of success before the event and honest evidence afterward. We won't promise behavior change we can't demonstrate.

Do you work with hybrid and virtual events?

Yes. Remote and onsite participants may take part differently, but they contribute to the same outcomes, run from the same production table.

Can you run voting for delegates and elections?

Yes. Digital voting for resolutions and elections, with results on screen as soon as voting closes and an alternative path for delegates who can't use the default method.

How do your platforms keep data secure?

We use established platforms, collect only the data your goals require, and agree with you on how it is handled and shared before the event.
